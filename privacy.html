Maple

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

No server, no collection

Maple has no server. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data, and Maple contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Where your data is kept

Your tasks, templates, settings and AI conversations are stored on your own devices. If you turn on sync, your tasks and templates are also stored in Maple's hidden app data folder in your own Google Drive, so that your devices can share them. If you turn on the calendar option, your tasks that have a due date are also copied to a calendar in your own Google Calendar.

Google sign-in, Google Drive and Google Calendar

Sync is optional and starts only when you sign in with Google in Maple's settings. Maple then asks for:

  • drive.appdata: to create, read and update Maple's own files in its hidden app data folder in your Google Drive. Maple cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • email and openid: to show which Google account is signed in.
  • calendar.app.created (only if you turn on the calendar option in Maple's settings): to create a calendar named "Maple" in your Google Calendar and to add, update and delete events in it for your tasks that have a due date (title, notes and date). Maple cannot see or change any of your other calendars or events.

This data is used only to sync your tasks between your own devices and to show them in your calendar. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising and not read by any person.

Maple's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI assistant

The AI assistant is optional. If you set up an AI connection, Maple sends your request, together with the task information it needs, directly from your device to the AI service you chose. That service's own privacy terms apply.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in Maple under Settings → Data to stop syncing. Turning off the calendar option there deletes the Maple calendar.
  • Remove Maple's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete Maple's synced files in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Maple → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Uninstalling the Android app deletes its data on the phone. On Windows, the uninstaller offers to delete Maple's data folder.
